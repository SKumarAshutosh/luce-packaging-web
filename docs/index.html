Flute profiles

A, B, C and E-flute available. E-flute for fine print and slim retail packs, C-flute for cushioning, B for stacking rigidity.

Paper grades

Kraft, semi-kraft, golden yellow, natural shade and virgin white-top liners. Water-resistant coating on request.

Testing

Bursting strength, GSM, cobb value and box compression checked per batch. Test certificates issued with dispatch.

Questions

Straight answers before you enquire

What is your minimum order quantity?

It depends on the size and whether printing is involved. Plain corrugated boxes start low, printed cartons need a larger run to justify plate and die costs. Tell us your monthly volume and we'll tell you honestly whether it's viable — or suggest a cheaper route.

Can you match a box I'm already buying?

Yes. Send a photo with a measuring tape across the length, or courier a sample. We'll reverse-engineer the size, ply and board grade and quote against it like-for-like.

How long does an order take?

Plain repeat orders typically dispatch within a few working days of confirmation. First-time printed jobs take longer because of die-making and proofing. You get a committed date with the quote, not a vague estimate.

Do you supply printed boxes in small quantities?

For low volumes we usually suggest a plain box with a single-colour flexo print or a printed label — you get branding without paying for offset lamination setup. We'll walk you through the cost difference.

Which ply should I choose?

Rough guide: under 10 kg and travelling short distances, 3-ply. E-commerce or general freight, 5-ply. Heavy, dense or export, 7-ply. Stacking height matters as much as weight — share both and we'll spec it properly.

Do you deliver outside your city?

Yes, through transporters for bulk consignments. Freight is quoted separately and transparently so you can compare against your own logistics.
